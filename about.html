Our Story

Fabulous Sushi opened in 2013 with the mission to bring restaurant-quality sushi to local supermarkets. Our team is passionate about freshness, creativity and customer service. Every roll is crafted by hand with care.

We believe sushi should be accessible to everyone. That's why we partner with ShopRite supermarkets across New Jersey to offer convenient sushi bars and grab-and-go options.
